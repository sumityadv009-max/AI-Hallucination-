# AI-Hallucination-
An AI-generated educational animation explaining AI hallucinations, why AI models can produce incorrect information, and how fact-checking and verification help improve the reliability of AI-generated answers.
Why AI Makes Things Up — AI Hallucination & Fact-Checking

About the Project-
This project is an AI-focused educational animation that explains why Artificial Intelligence systems sometimes generate incorrect, misleading, or fabricated information, a phenomenon known as AI hallucination.

Through animated visuals and a step-by-step explanation, the video demonstrates the importance of fact-checking, source verification, and validating AI-generated information before using it.

Key Concepts-
* AI Hallucinations: When AI generates information that is inaccurate or unsupported.
* Why AI Makes Mistakes: AI-generated answers can sound confident even when they are incorrect.
* Fact-Checking: Comparing AI responses against reliable sources.
* Verification: Checking important claims before trusting or sharing them.
* Responsible AI Usage: Using AI critically rather than assuming every answer is correct.

Project Format-
* Type: Educational AI Animation
* Topic: Artificial Intelligence and AI Hallucinations
* Visual Style: Animated infographic
* Purpose: AI awareness and digital education

Project Objective-
To help viewers understand the limitations of AI-generated content and develop the habit of verifying important information before relying on it.

Learning Outcomes-
* Understand the concept of AI hallucinations.
* Recognize the limitations of generative AI.
* Learn why independent verification matters.
* Develop responsible AI research and fact-checking practices.

Important Note-
AI-generated information should not automatically be treated as factual. Verify important claims using reliable, authoritative sources.
